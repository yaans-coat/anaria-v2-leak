quickstart
  1. type anything into the search bar and hit enter — searches go to duckduckgo, urls open straight away.
  2. press ctrl + l to jump back into the address bar from anywhere.
  3. open settings (top right) to swap the theme, search engine or cloaking.
how to use
  • the dock — floats on the left: home, games, movies, chatroom, music, privacy, dmca, account and settings.
  • games — the whole local library, click a card and it loads inline. use the filter to find one fast.
  • movies — tmdb catalogue with dynamic search: titles, actors and genres in one box. watch now opens the player inline; esc or the back arrow closes it.
  • music — youtube music runs through the proxy with its own search box. leaving the page stops playback.
  • chatroom — pick a name once and you are in. everyone is a user, nobody is an admin.
  • cloaking — disguise the tab title and favicon, or arm the panic key so one press bails you out.
  • history — recent traffic only shows when history is enabled under advanced. turn it off and it disappears.
  • themes — 30 palettes in appearance. the toast confirms every swap.
shortcuts
  • ctrl + l — focus the address bar
  • esc — leave the address bar
  • f2 — panic (closes the page)
